  1. On the Minimal Orbital Scheme O‾min⁡∩n+\overline{\mathcal O}_{\min}\cap\mathfrak n^{+}Omin​∩n+

    GPT-6 Astra · Communicated and edited by Boming Jia

    PDFGitHubv1.0.0
    Abstract
    Let g\mathfrak gg be a complex simple Lie algebra, let n+\mathfrak n^{+}n+ be the nilradical of a Borel subalgebra, and let Omin⁡\mathcal O_{\min}Omin​ be the minimal nonzero nilpotent orbit. We prove that O‾min⁡∩n+\overline{\mathcal O}_{\min}\cap\mathfrak n^{+}Omin​∩n+ is generically reduced in every type. In simply laced type, it is reduced and Cohen–Macaulay. In non-simply laced type, it is nonreduced and, being generically reduced, fails Serre's condition (S1)(S_1)(S1​). In simply laced type, a quadratic Gröbner basis degenerates its coordinate ring to the Stanley–Reisner ring of the positive-root order complex. As a consequence, minimal scheme-theoretic Springer fibers are generically reduced in every type, reduced and Cohen–Macaulay in simply laced type, and nonreduced in non-simply laced type.

  2. The Minimal Leaf of T∗(G/U)affT^*(G/U)^{\mathrm{aff}}T∗(G/U)aff

    GPT-6 Astra · Communicated and edited by Boming Jia

    PDFGitHubv1.0.1
    Abstract
    Let GGG be a simply connected simple complex algebraic group, and let UUU be a maximal unipotent subgroup. Assume that GGG is not of type A1A_1A1​ or A2A_2A2​. We construct a GGG-equivariant closed Poisson embedding of the minimal nilpotent orbit closure into T∗(G/U)affT^*(G/U)^{\mathrm{aff}}T∗(G/U)aff. The image of the minimal orbit is a symplectic leaf contained in the closure of every positive-dimensional symplectic leaf. It is the unique positive-dimensional leaf of smallest dimension.

Research interests

My research interests lie in geometric representation theory, especially the interactions of representation theory with complex algebraic (symplectic) geometry. More specifically I study symplectic singularities, complex coadjoint orbits, quiver varieties, flag varieties, and (affine) Springer fibers.
